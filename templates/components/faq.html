Questions before you buy

How do I get the e-book after I buy it?

It's a digital download you can read on your phone, tablet, or computer — no physical copy is shipped.

Do I need special ingredients or equipment?

No. The whole book is built around a short list of staples — oats, beans, lentils, rice, seasonal vegetables — and basic stovetop or oven cooking. Most recipes use 5–10 ingredients.

Is this a restrictive diet plan?

No. The goal isn't to eat less — it's to waste less, choose better, and get more value from every meal. There are no elimination rules or extreme calorie targets.

Will the recipes work outside the US?

Yes. Costs and savings are shown across multiple currencies, and the seasonal shopping guidance is built to adapt to your local produce and climate.

How is this different from recipes I could find for free?

It's a full system, not a recipe list: a weekly budget target, a shopping list built to hit it, a cooking order that minimizes waste, and a way to track what you're actually saving over time.
