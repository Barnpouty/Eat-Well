What's inside

Six chapters that move from mindset to grocery list to a full week of cooking, plus a bonus chapter of fast recipes built from the same ingredients you already bought.

  1. 01

    The Eat-Well-Spend-Less System

    Batch cooking, leftovers, ingredient repetition, zero-waste thinking, and why cheap food doesn't mean unhealthy food.

  2. 02

    The $45–$50 Weekly Framework

    The financial backbone: budget targets, a core shopping list, pantry essentials, and cost boundaries that prevent overspending.

  3. 03

    Smart Grocery Shopping & Spending Control

    Shopping lists, store layout, generic brands, sales, bulk buying, unit-price math, and impulse control.

  4. 04

    Practical Meal Planning & Recipes

    A 7-day meal plan, a time-efficient structure, and breakfasts, lunches, dinners, and snacks that flex around what you have.

  5. 05

    Nutrition That Keeps Energy Stable

    Low-GI principles, fiber-and-protein pairing, and how to eat for steady energy without expensive "health" foods.

  6. 06

    Execution, Tracking & Long-Term Results

    Sunday prep, weekly execution, ingredient swaps, and turning this into a permanent habit rather than a 30-day challenge.

  7. Bonus

    The Budget Home Chef's Guide

    20 extra recipes under $3 a serving, made entirely from your master shopping list — no extra grocery trips.
