The number that matters

Everything in the book points at one weekly target: keep your grocery spend at $45–$50 for one to two people, built from seasonal produce, dried staples, and store-brand basics.

Hit that target consistently and most households save somewhere between $10 and $20 a week against their usual bill. Keep it up at $50 saved a week, and that's $2,600 back over a year — money the book's own tracking pages are built to help you watch add up.
